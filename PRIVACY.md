# Phomic privacy policy

*Last updated: 21 November 2026*

Phomic makes your iPhone a microphone for your own Windows PC. It doesn't collect any personal data.

- **Your audio** goes straight from your iPhone to the PC you pick, over your local network (Wi-Fi),
  Bluetooth or a USB cable. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else, and Phomic doesn't record or store it.
- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.** The only thing the apps ask the internet is whether there's a newer
  version of Phomic: the iPhone app asks the App Store (Apple), the PC app asks GitHub, now and then. That request
  contains nothing about you or your audio, and Phomic stores nothing about it; like any web request, it shows the
  server your IP address. When you click *Update*, the PC app downloads the new installer from GitHub the same way.
  (The PC app also opens web pages in your browser when you click a link in it.)
- **Feedback:** *Give feedback* (in the PC app's window, and in the iPhone app's Settings) opens a new issue on
  GitHub in your browser, with the app's version and your system's version filled in. Nothing is sent unless you
  submit it there, and issues on GitHub are public.
- **Settings stay on your devices.** On the iPhone, Phomic keeps its own settings (for example saved pairing codes
  and whether the Live Activity is on); deleting the app removes them. On the PC, settings are kept in the Windows
  registry under `HKCU\Software\Phomic`; uninstalling removes them.
- **Permissions:** *Microphone* to stream it, *Local Network* to find and connect to your PC over Wi-Fi, and
  *Bluetooth* only if you use Bluetooth. USB needs no permission.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/Nordohm/Phomic/issues>.
