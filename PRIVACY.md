# Phomic privacy policy

*Last updated: 16 November 2026*

Phomic makes your iPhone a microphone for your own Windows PC. It doesn't collect any personal data.

- **Your audio** goes straight from your iPhone to the PC you pick, over your local network (Wi-Fi) or
  Bluetooth. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else, and Phomic doesn't record or store it.
- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.** The apps don't contact any server on the internet. (The PC app
  only opens web pages in your browser when you click a link in it.)
- **Settings stay on your devices.** On the iPhone, Phomic keeps its own settings (for example saved pairing codes
  and whether the Live Activity is on); deleting the app removes them. On the PC, settings are kept in the Windows
  registry under `HKCU\Software\Phomic`; uninstalling removes them.
- **Permissions:** *Microphone* to stream it, *Local Network* to find and connect to your PC over Wi-Fi, and
  *Bluetooth* only if you use Bluetooth.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/Nordohm/Phomic/issues>.
